The Record

Origin Story

that's me — the curious one in the room

I got into economics for maths and research and stayed for the human behaviour: why money moves, what demand actually is, where it comes from. The curiosity never stayed in the classroom. I studied markets obsessively, and college expanded the syllabus: movements around women's empowerment and literature, fieldwork and research, AIESEC, and co-founding Felicity, a creative venture we ran into profitability. I consulted pro bono for NGOs on grants, donors and SDG programs, taught in Delhi through Teach For India and in Taiwan, got selected for HBS Peek, and interned at a YC startup that converted me to a full-time offer. Then the real syllabus: crypto growth through a bull and a bear market, fintech PMM, founding GTM for a unified data stack platform that pivoted into an open source data replication tool. Biggest lesson: markets run on trust, and trust begins with understanding the person on the other side of the market.
