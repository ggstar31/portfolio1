The Full Story

The "Why" behind the execution

A 2-minute read. Both halves of the cycle, one community.

I joined as an intern at the top of a bull run. By the time my full-time offer was converted, the bear had arrived on schedule. Fourteen months across both halves of the cycle, growing a trading app through the exact period users had the least reason to trade.

The intern brief was warm leads: one-time and infrequent visitors who'd touched the platform but never invested. The channel I ran was webinars, education before conversion, and it worked well enough to become the template I'd later carry to developer GTM.

Teaching converts skeptics that pitching cannot reach.

The observation

Then came the observation that turned into the real project. The same students kept showing up, and they brought friends.

Students weren't a side audience; they were the future target audience arriving early: interested in crypto now, employed and investing in a few years. So instead of treating them as low-value leads, we built them a home. That became WAGMI, invite-only, and it was the internship project I owned completely.

ISB partnered first. Then the flywheel: IITs, NITs, IIMs, BITS, MDI, every tier-1 institution, 50+ student ambassadors, and 5,000+ members in 8 months. The design goal from day one was a community that runs as a system, not a fan club: student ambassadors running their own chapters.

The India business

The full-time promotion moved me to the India business team: influencer marketing and brand partnerships. This is where the craft got quantitative: ROI, ROAS, CTR, engagement, conversion rate, cost per acquisition, tracked per campaign, running partnerships with some of India's biggest finance creators.

Alongside: brand partnerships end to end with Economic Times, Zepto, CRED, Decrypt and KoinX, BTL activations across Bengaluru offices and co-working spaces, and retention built on data, weekly and monthly cohorts, DAU/MAU, drop-off analysis aimed at the exact leak points.
