The Money Club website and brochure

The Money Club lets people pool money with a group of verified members, a digital version of the savings circles that are common across India. I worked on the website copy and this brochure, which tells the story through Umesh, one of the 400 million lower middle class Indians that banks find hard to lend to.

48 Hours to 1 Minute: How Insurance Tech Startup Automated 300 Reports with Datazip

A Mumbai health and insurance tech company was waiting up to 48 hours for its reports because of slow, disconnected pipelines. This is the story of how Datazip, OLake's parent company, brought that down to one minute across 250 to 300 reports. I co-wrote it with the team, built on our conversations with the customer.
